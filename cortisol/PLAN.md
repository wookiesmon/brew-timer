## What actually works (ranked by impact)
1. **Sleep 7–9 h at fixed times.** Two weeks of short sleep raised visceral fat by 11% in a Mayo Clinic trial.
2. **A modest calorie deficit** from whole food, with protein at 1.2–1.6 g/kg and 25–38 g fiber a day.
3. **Aerobic exercise (Zone 2 + intervals) and strength training.** 150–300 min of cardio a week plus 2–3 strength sessions.
4. **No alcohol, no sugary drinks, little ultra-processed food.**
5. **A daily downshift:** 5 min of cyclic sighing or 5.5-breaths/min breathing, morning light, and evening darkness.
6. **Supplements: the smallest lever.** At best about 1–3 cm off the waist in 12 weeks.

## Weekly training
- **Mon:** Strength A
- **Tue:** Zone 2, 40–50 min
- **Wed:** Strength B
- **Thu:** Intervals (from week 3) or Zone 2
- **Fri:** Strength C (from week 5) or Zone 2, plus yoga
- **Sat:** Long Zone 2 hike, 60–90 min
- **Sun:** Rest

Swap any hard day for an easy one when HRV is low, resting HR is up 5+ bpm, or you slept badly. Weeks 4 and 8 are lighter (about 60% of usual volume).

## The three phases
### Phase 1: Reset (weeks 1–4)
- **Day 1:** measure waist and weight, take photos, do the PSS-10, and get baseline labs.
- Build the daily rhythm above.
- Tier 1 supplements only.
- 12 h eating window.
- Strength 2× a week.

### Phase 2: Build (weeks 5–8)
- Strength 3× a week, plus 1 interval session.
- 10–11 h eating window.
- Sauna 2–3× a week if your heart is healthy.
- If cleared, add Tier 2 supplements one every 1–2 weeks.
- Recheck ALT and TSH at weeks 6–8 if you're on ashwagandha.

### Phase 3: Settle (weeks 9–12)
- 200–300 min of cardio a week, intervals up to 2× a week, strength 3× a week.
- Stop ashwagandha by week 12.
- **Week 12:** repeat labs, PSS-10, photos and measurements.
- Plan maintenance: keep about 80% of the habits.

## Checkpoints & 12-week targets
Checkpoints fall on days 1, 28, 56 and 84. The app reminds you.

| Metric | Target by week 12 |
|---|---|
| Waist | −3 to −8 cm. Waist-to-height ratio heading under 0.50. |
| Weight | −0.25 to −1% a week (7-day average) |
| PSS-10 | −3 to −5 points |
| Resting HR | −2 to −5 bpm |
| HRV | Weekly average +5–10% |
| Sleep | 7 h or more on at least 5 nights a week |

## Supplements
### Tier 1: weeks 1+
- **Magnesium glycinate:** 200 mg before bed, rising to 300 mg from week 3.
- **Omega-3:** 2 g EPA+DHA with dinner.
- **D3 + K2:** 2,000 IU + 100 µg with breakfast.
- **Psyllium:** 3–5 g before lunch and before dinner.
- **L-theanine:** 200 mg with coffee.
- **Creatine:** 5 g a day (optional).

### Tier 2: week 5+, only if cleared
- **Ashwagandha KSM-66:** 300 mg twice a day for 8 weeks at most. Check ALT and TSH.
- **Glycine:** 3 g before bed.
- **Probiotic:** *L. helveticus* R0052 + *B. longum* R0175.
- **Saffron:** 14 mg twice a day. Not with SSRIs or SNRIs.

### Tier 3: only through your doctor
Berberine, myo-inositol, melatonin, GLP-1 drugs or metformin.

### Skip
Green tea extract, CLA, apple cider vinegar, chromium, NMN/NR, resveratrol, garcinia, "cortisol blockers", DHEA, adrenal glandulars, coffee enemas.

## Labs (baseline + week 12)
Draw fasting, 07:00–09:00, with no biotin for 72 h beforehand.
- AM cortisol
- Fasting glucose + insulin
- HbA1c
- Lipids (± ApoB)
- hs-CRP
- TSH + free T4
- Vitamin D
- Ferritin + CBC
- CMP (liver + kidney)
- B12/folate
- Testosterone + SHBG
- Uric acid
- Blood pressure

## See a doctor if
- You have purple stretch marks wider than 1 cm, easy bruising, a round red face, a fat pad between the shoulders, or weak thighs when standing from a chair. These are possible signs of Cushing's syndrome.
- You develop new high blood pressure or diabetes.
- You have signs of sleep apnea or persistent low mood.
- Your waist hasn't changed after 8 honest weeks.
