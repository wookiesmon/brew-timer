# 05: The 84-Day Film Playbook

*Content Studio · prepared 2026-10-27 · companion to [04 Growth & roadmap](04-growth-roadmap.md) §1.2–1.9 and [03 Legal](03-legal.md) §2*

**What this is:** the production bible for the founder's on-camera 12-week (84-day) run of the Ebb program. It is the anchor of the zero-budget go-to-market in [BUSINESS_PLAN.md](BUSINESS_PLAN.md) §5.

**Who is on camera:** the founder, 51, no medications, bilingual EN/KR.
- **Weekdays:** wakes 08:00, breakfast and getting ready 08:00–10:00, works 10:00–18:00, eats lunch out at 12:00 most days, sleeps at 00:00.
- **Evenings:** yoga at 18:30 three times a week; dinner at 19:00 on the other days.

**Launch order:** US/English first, then Korea, then Spanish-speaking markets. Every shoot is captured so that it can be subtitled in Korean later (§9).

> **Ground rules that override everything below.**
> 1. Ebb is a wellness habit program. The Calm Score is a habit score, **not a cortisol test**.
> 2. Never say the app or any habit "lowers cortisol", "burns belly fat", "cures", "treats" or "detoxes" anything.
> 3. The founder owns the app, so **every video that shows or names Ebb says "I made this app."**
> 4. No supplement doses on camera.
> 5. One person's numbers are not a promise.
>
> See §7. This is not legal advice; have counsel review §7 before the series starts.

## 0. What the 2026 research says (and how it shaped this plan)

Sources are mostly creator-economy blogs, not platform documentation, so these are working assumptions to test against our own analytics (§8). Links are in the Sources section at the end.

| Finding | What we do with it |

| **Watch-through and shares beat raw views.** On Instagram, DM sends and shares appear to drive non-follower reach. Completion rate is described as the top Shorts signal. | Keep most videos short enough to finish. Write a "send this to…" moment into myth-busting posts. |

Under 90 s reportedly holds about 50% retention; over 90 s about 35%. | **Default 25–45 s.** Weekly recaps run 60–90 s. Nothing over 90 s in weeks 1–3. Test length as a variable after post 21. |
| **Most viewers watch muted.** | Burned-in captions on every video. On-screen text carries the story without sound. |
| **Cadence advice is wide:** TikTok 1–3 a day, Reels 3–5 a week, Shorts 2–3 a week up to daily. Every source says quality beats volume. | **1 video a day**, natively uploaded to all three, as [04](04-growth-roadmap.md) §1.2 plans. If quality slips, drop to 5 a week; never post filler. |

| **Cortisol niche:** doctors and mainstream press keep debunking "cortisol face" and "cortisol cocktails". | **Over-50 transformation:** we found no hard 2026 trend data. Adjacent signals: the over-50 stories that resonate are about consistency, sleep, protein, walking and fewer, smarter workouts, not extremes. | Lean into "ordinary 51-year-old with a desk job and lunch out", not "shredded at 51". ## 1. Series concept and title options

### 1.1 Concept

> **One ordinary 51-year-old with a desk job, lunch out and a midnight bedtime runs a free 12-week stress-and-waist program. He measures everything on camera, posts every number including the bad ones, and fixes the app he built when it gets something wrong.**

Why this works:
- **The stakes are measurable.** Tape, scale, resting HR and PSS-10 give a real "will it move?" open loop that runs for 84 days.
- **He's relatable, not aspirational.** He eats out, works 8 hours and goes to bed at midnight. Viewers see themselves in him.
- **It's honest by design.** It is the anti-hype voice in a hype niche, which is also our legal safety net.
- **There's a built-in founder arc.** He made the app. Viewers watch both the body and the product change.

### 1.2 Title options

| **1 ★** | **51, Stressed, and Measuring Everything** | **쉰한 살, 스트레스까지 다 재봅니다** | Age plus emotion plus method in five words. Promises data, not miracles. Works as a handle-style series tag ("51 & Measuring"). |

| 3 | The Cortisol Trend, Minus the Nonsense | 코르티솔 유행, 과장 빼고 84일 | Strong positioning. Risks reading as a cortisol claim; better as a sub-series for myth posts. |
| 4 | No Hype, Just Tape | 과장 없이, 줄자로 | Great on-screen tag line. Too abstract as the main title. |
| 5 | The Calm Waist Experiment | 스트레스 뱃살 84일 실험 | "Experiment" framing invites comments. "Calm waist" is a bit soft. |

**Favourite: "51, Stressed, and Measuring Everything".** Use **"No Hype, Just Tape"** as the recurring end-card line.

- **On-screen series tag:** `51 & MEASURING · DAY 12/84`

## 2. Day 0 setup checklist

**Day 0 is the day before Day 1.** Start Day 1 on a **Monday** so the weekly rhythm lines up. The checkpoints then fall on Mon D1 and on the Sundays of D28, D56 and D84.

- **Day 0 evening (20 min):** build the set, test the frame and lock the settings.
- **Day 1 at 08:05:** shoot the baseline, fasted, after the bathroom, before coffee and breakfast.

### 2.1 Before Day 0 (one-time)

- [ ] **Doctor's OK.** Tell the doctor the plan: diet changes, more exercise, the supplements PLAN.md lists. Ask about baseline blood tests (PLAN.md lists what to discuss). Film only the line "I checked with my doctor first", never the visit itself.
- [ ] **Accounts:**
  - TikTok **business account** (needed for ManyChat and the content-disclosure toggle)
  - Instagram **professional/creator account**
  - YouTube channel
  - Use the same handle everywhere (e.g. `@51andmeasuring` or `@ebb.founder`; check availability).
- [ ] **Bio and link page live** (§6.3). Email tool (Kit free tier) and the PDF lead magnet ready ([04](04-growth-roadmap.md) §1.6).
- [ ] **ManyChat** connected to IG with the `RESET` keyword flow tested from a second account (§6.1).
- [ ] **Turn off camera location** (iOS: Settings → Privacy → Location → Camera → Never; Android: camera settings → location tags off). Check one exported file's metadata to confirm there's no GPS.
- [ ] Create a **"51 Masters"** folder in cloud storage, with subfolders per week. Raw files are named `D012_Fri_stress_raw.mov`.

### 2.2 Gear for under $100 (phone-only)

Prices are approximate US street prices in late 2026; check before buying. He already has the phone.

| Item | Why | ≈ Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Tripod with phone clamp** (adjustable ~40–160 cm, Bluetooth remote included) | Locked framing for measure shots is the whole point. The remote lets him start recording alone. | $20–25 |
| **Wireless clip-on mic**, USB-C or Lightning to match the phone (2 transmitters is a nice-to-have) | Clear voice on the lunch walk and in the restaurant. Most viewers watch muted, but those who listen leave if audio is bad. | $20–30 |
| **Small LED light** (bi-colour, 5600K setting, clamps to the tripod) | **Fixed lighting** for checkpoint photos regardless of weather. Set it to one brightness/temperature and tape over the dial. | $15–25 |
| **Locking body tape measure** (self-retracting, e.g. the MyoTape style) | Same tension every time. Readable on camera. | $6–10 |
| **Coloured gaffer/painter's tape** | Floor marks for feet and tripod legs; wall mark for the camera height. | $5–8 |
| **Total** | | **≈ $66–98** |

Already owned or free:
- **Bathroom scale.** Any digital one, always the same scale on the same hard floor spot.
- **Plain wall.** Light grey or white, no art, no windows, no mirrors.
- **One "checkpoint shirt".** A fitted plain t-shirt, worn only for checkpoints.
- **Wearable for resting HR, if he has one.** Otherwise use the 60-second pulse count (§2.3).

### 2.3 Baseline shoot list (Day 1, 08:05–08:20; repeat on D28, D56, D84)

Shoot in this order. Every checkpoint repeats it **exactly**: same time, same spot, same shirt, same light, same order.

| # | Shot | How | On-screen output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Slate** | Hold the phone's date screen (or a card reading "Day 1 · 08:05") to camera for 2 s. | Proof of date, for honesty. |
| 2 | **Waist tape on camera** | Find the midpoint between the lowest rib and the top of the hip bone. Stand relaxed, feet together. Measure at the end of a normal breath out, without sucking in. Measure **3 times** and use the middle number. Push the camera in, or cut to a phone close-up, so the number is readable. | `Waist: 98.5 cm (38.8 in)`. US audience: show **inches first, cm second**. |
| 3 | **Waist-to-height ratio** | Waist ÷ height. Say it once; PLAN.md's target is to head under 0.50. | `Waist ÷ height = 0.56` |
| 4 | **Scale** | Same scale and floor tile. Film feet and display in one shot, with no face in it so it's reusable. | `Weight: 182.4 lb (82.7 kg)`. From D8 on, report the **7-day average**, not the single reading. |
| 5 | **Photos: front, side, back** | **Tripod at the taped floor mark, 2.0 m from the wall. Lens at navel height (taped wall mark). 1× lens, never 0.5×. LED at the fixed setting, ceiling light off, curtains the same.** Arms relaxed at the sides, normal breath out, neutral face. Take 3 stills plus a slow 3 s 360° turn on video. | Kept as private masters. What gets posted is in §7.4. |
| 6 | **Resting heart rate** | Before coffee, after sitting quietly for 5 min. Wearable reading, or count the wrist pulse for a full 60 s on camera (phone stopwatch in frame). | `Resting HR: 68 bpm` |
| 7 | **PSS-10** | Fill it in inside the Ebb app. Film the **score screen only**, not the full questionnaire text. | `Stress score (PSS-10): 21/40`. Add the context line "0–13 lower · 14–26 moderate · 27–40 higher". |
| 8 | **Calm Score** | Screen-record the app's Calm Score after Day 1 check-in. | `Calm Score: 52 (a habit score, not a cortisol test)` |
| 9 | **Talking head (30 s, unscripted)** | "How I feel today: energy, sleep, stress, belt notch, one sentence." | Raw material for the D84 side-by-side "then vs now" voice clip. |
| 10 | **B-roll** | The tape in hand, stepping on the scale, the app's home screen, coffee being made (to show before coffee). | Cutaways for the whole series. |

Log all numbers in the Ebb app **and** in a private sheet (the sheet also feeds §8 metrics).

### 2.4 The consistent framing template

**Camera settings, locked for the whole series:**
- 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920 (4K if storage allows), **30 fps**, main 1× lens.
- Exposure/focus lock: tap and hold.
- HDR off for checkpoint shots, so the look stays the same from day to day.

**Three framings only:**

| Name | Use | Framing |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Talking head** | Hooks, recaps, myths | Lens at eye level. Eyes on the upper-third line. Head and chest in frame. ~1 m away. Plain wall or a calm home corner (the same one every time). |
| **B · Measure** | Tape, scale, photos | The taped floor mark, 2.0 m, lens at navel height, full body, LED fixed. Never moved between checkpoints. |
| **C · Walk-and-talk / Table** | Lunch walk, restaurant, yoga | Handheld selfie at arm's length, phone slightly above eye level. On the table: phone propped at plate height, top-down plate shot for 2 s. |

**Safe zones.** Keep all text and key action out of:
- the **bottom ~20%** (captions/UI),
- the **right ~15%** (like/comment buttons),
- the **top ~10%** (search/handle).

**Persistent overlays:**
- Top-left: `51 & MEASURING · DAY 12/84`, in a sage pill on sand text, the same font every time.
- On any frame showing the app: a small `I made this app` tag (§7.1).
- End card (1 s): `No hype, just tape.`

**Caption style.** Burned-in, 2 lines max, 5–7 words per line, high contrast, key numbers in bold or highlighted.

### 2.5 Privacy checklist (run before every upload)

**Never in frame:**
- [ ] Home address, house number, street signs, mail, packages, delivery labels or the front door from outside.
- [ ] Views from windows that identify the building or street. A car **licence plate**.
- [ ] The **workplace**: company name, logo, badge, lanyard, office, laptop screens, colleagues, Slack/email.
- [ ] **Kids**, or any family member who hasn't explicitly agreed. No family photos on walls in the background.
- [ ] **Lab reports or doctor documents with identifiers**: name, date of birth, patient/MRN number, barcodes or QR codes, clinic name, address or phone, physician name, insurance ID. If a lab result is shown at all, retype the single value as an on-screen graphic. Never film the paper or the portal. Never feature an AM-cortisol lab value as an "outcome" (§7.2).
- [ ] Phone notifications in screen recordings. Turn on **Do Not Disturb** before recording.
- [ ] Wi-Fi network names, calendar entries, contact names, other apps' data.
- [ ] **Other people at yoga or in restaurants.** Film only himself, film at the studio only with permission, and blur any face that appears.

**Patterns that reveal location:**
- [ ] Post lunch and yoga videos **after** he has left, never live.
- [ ] Rotate restaurant shots. Don't show the same storefront near the office every week.
- [ ] Don't name the yoga studio or neighbourhood.

**Health data:**
- [ ] Share only the numbers he is happy to stay public forever.
- [ ] No screenshots of the phone's Health app showing other data.

**Final step:** scrub every frame in 1× playback, at full screen, before posting. Have a second person check the checkpoint posts.

## 3. Weekly content rhythm

### 3.1 How filming fits his day (≤15 min of filming a day)

| Window | Filming used for | Max |
|---|---|---|
| **08:00–08:20**, fasted, before breakfast | Measure shots, resting HR, talking-head hooks, morning breathing | 10–15 min |
| **12:00–12:40**, lunch out plus a 10–15 min walk after | Plate, ordering, walk-and-talk | 5–10 min |
| **18:30**, yoga (3×/week; assumed **Tue/Thu/Sat**, swap as needed) | 2–3 B-roll clips before or after class, plus one line to camera on the way out | 3–5 min |

Rule: **film only one window a day, two on lighter days.** Editing is separate (§9), about 20 min a day with AI tools, or a 2-hour Sunday batch.

### 3.2 One recurring format per day

| Day | Format | Shot in | Length | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | **Monday Measure** | 08:05, framing B + A | 25–35 s | Tape, scale (7-day avg) and one honest sentence. The spine of the series. |
| **Tue** | **Build Log: "I made this app"** | 08:30 screen recording + 1 line in framing A; yoga B-roll at 18:30 | 30–45 s | What he changed in Ebb this week and why. Privacy/offline demos. Always carries the founder disclosure. |
| **Wed** | **What I Eat Eating Out** | 12:00 lunch (framing C) + walk | 25–40 s | Ordering a protein-first lunch at real US restaurant types. The single most relatable office-worker format. |
| **Thu** | **Myth or Evidence** | 12:20 lunch walk, walk-and-talk (framing C) | 30–45 s | Rate a trending cortisol claim 0–10 using studies. Stitch/duet friendly. Built to be sent and shared. |
| **Fri** | **Stress Score** | 08:05, framing A + app screen | 25–40 s | Weekly Calm Score + resting HR + a 1–10 self-rating. The PSS-10 only at checkpoints (it asks about "the last month"). Includes 1 min of the breathing pacer as B-roll. |
| **Sat** | **51 & Bendy** (yoga/recovery/sleep) | 18:30 yoga B-roll (framing C) + one line | 20–35 s | Yoga progress, sleep and recovery. "What 51-year-old hamstrings feel like in week 3." Lowest-effort day. |
| **Sun** | **Week Recap** | 09:00 talking head (framing A), up to 15 min | 60–90 s | Week N: numbers chart, what worked, what didn't, next week's focus. Becomes the YouTube long-form building block and the KR backlog. |

**Weekend lunch:** Sat/Sun lunch can be home-cooked. Optional bonus B-roll only; never required.

### 3.3 Posting times and cross-posting

- **Times:** post once a day around **07:30 ET** (people scrolling on their commute) or **12:15 ET** (lunch break). A/B the slot for weeks 1–3, then lock the winner. Schedule with Postiz, Buffer or Metricool, but **upload natively** to each platform.
- **Same master to TikTok, Reels and Shorts**, each with its own caption and title (§9).
- **Instagram Stories** daily (zero-edit): a poll ("Did my waist move? Yes/No") on Mon, a "your lunch?" question box on Wed, the link sticker on Sun.
- **YouTube long-form** every 2 weeks: stitch two Sunday recaps plus extra B-roll into 6–10 min ("Weeks 1–2: the boring truth").

## 5. Scripts for the first 14 days (English)

**Conventions:**
- **[X]** = fill in his real number. Never pre-write results.
- Times in brackets are seconds.
- **OST** = on-screen text.
- Every post carries the `51 & MEASURING · DAY N/84` pill top-left.
- Each caption ends with the tag line and 3–5 hashtags.
- Any post that shows the app carries the disclosure **spoken + OST** + the TikTok "Your brand" toggle.

### Day 1 (Mon) · Monday Measure · "The numbers I'm scared to post"
- **Shoot:** 08:05, framings B + A. Full baseline (§2.3). **Length:** 35 s
- **Hook [0–3]:** (tape already around waist, framing B) "I'm 51, and these are the numbers I'm scared to post."
  OST: `51. Stressed. Measuring everything for 84 days.`
- **Beats:**
  - [3–10] Waist close-up → `Waist: [X] in / [X] cm`. "That's my waist, measured properly. No sucking in."
  - [10–15] Scale → `Weight: [X] lb`.
  - [15–20] Resting HR count → `Resting HR: [X]`. PSS-10 screen → `Stress score: [X]/40`.
  - [20–28] Framing A: "Desk job. Lunch out every day. Bed at midnight. I'm doing a free 12-week stress-and-waist program and filming every number, even the bad weeks. I checked with my doctor first."
  - [28–33] "Every Monday, same tape, same spot."
- **CTA [33–35]:** "Follow to see if any of this moves." OST: `Day 28 = first check. Follow along.`
- **Caption:** `Day 1 of 84. Waist, weight, resting heart rate and stress score, all on camera. One normal 51-year-old, not a promise. No hype, just tape. #over50 #stress #waistline #84days #buildinpublic`
- **Pinned comment:** `Questions you want me to track? Drop them. I'll answer with data on Sunday.`

### Day 2 (Tue) · Build Log · "An app that doesn't want your email"
- **Shoot:** 08:30. Screen recording + 1 line in framing A. **Length:** 40 s

  - [3–12] Screen recording: airplane mode on → Ebb opens → logs yesterday's waist. "No signup. No account. Your data stays on your phone. It even works offline."

  - [22–32] "It's a 12-week plan: sleep, protein, walking, breathing, and a tape measure. I'm doing it myself, on camera, for 84 days."
  - [32–37] **Disclosure, spoken:** "Full disclosure: I made this app, so I'm biased. That's why I'm showing you my real numbers."
- **CTA [37–40]:** "It's called Ebb. Link in bio, free." OST: `Ebb · free · no signup · I'm the founder`
- **Caption:** `I'm the founder of Ebb (yes, I'm biased). Free, offline, no account. The Calm Score is a habit score, not a cortisol test. #buildinpublic #indiedev #privacy #over50`

## 7. Compliance guardrails for creator content

> Not legal advice. These rules turn [03 Legal](03-legal.md) §2 into on-camera habits. Have counsel review them once before Day 1.

### 7.1 FTC: he owns the app, so he discloses it

- **Material connection.** The founder has a financial interest in Ebb. Under the FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR 255), any video that **shows, names or recommends Ebb** must disclose that clearly and conspicuously, in the video itself.
- **The standard disclosure:**
  - **Spoken:** "I made this app" or "Ebb is my app."
  - **On-screen:** an `I made this app` tag for at least 3 s while the app is on screen.
  - **Caption:** "I'm the founder of Ebb" in the first line, above the "more" fold.
- **Platform toggles** (verify each platform's current definitions):
  - **TikTok:** Content disclosure → "Your brand" (labels the post "Promotional content"). This is locked after posting, so set it before publishing.
  - **YouTube:** tick "includes paid promotion" on videos that promote the app, if YouTube's current policy covers own-product promotion.
  - **Instagram:** the "Paid partnership" label is for third-party deals. For own-brand, the spoken and on-screen disclosure carries it.
- **Hashtags alone are not enough.** `#founder` buried among 10 hashtags doesn't count.
- **Reviews and testimonials** (16 CFR 465):
  - Never seed, buy or AI-generate comments, reviews or "user stories".
  - Never delete negative comments for being negative. Remove only spam, abuse or medical misinformation.
- **Korea (later):** the KFTC endorsement guideline requires disclosure in Korean, near the start, e.g. "제가 만든 앱입니다 (광고 아님, 개발자 본인)". Have Korean counsel confirm the exact wording ([03](03-legal.md) §2.3).

### 7.2 Banned claims

Never say, write, caption or imply any of these, in any language:
- "lowers cortisol" / "reduces cortisol" / "balances hormones" / "cortisol reset" *as an outcome*
- "burns belly fat" / "melts visceral fat" / "flattens your stomach" / "targets belly fat"
- "cures", "treats", "heals", "reverses" or "prevents" (stress, anxiety, depression, insomnia, diabetes, Cushing's, "adrenal fatigue")
- "detox"
- "clinically proven app" / "doctor-approved", unless a named, contracted advisor has actually reviewed it
- "you'll lose X inches" / any guaranteed result / "in just 12 weeks"
- "my Calm Score shows my cortisol dropped". **The Calm Score is a habit score, not a cortisol test.**

**Lab tests on camera:** if he shares his own repeat tests, never present an AM cortisol value as proof the program or app "worked". Better: don't feature cortisol labs at all.

### 7.3 Supplements on camera

- **No doses as advice.** Don't say "take 300 mg of X". Don't show labels with doses in a way that reads as a recommendation.
- If he takes something, the only allowed framing is: *"I take [name]. I asked my doctor first. I'm not telling you to; talk to your doctor."*
- **Always say "talk to your doctor"** whenever a supplement is mentioned. Never name products on the PLAN.md "Skip" list except to debunk them.
- **No affiliate links in the 84-day series.** That keeps every video clear of the advertising-claim exposure in [03](03-legal.md) §2.1–2.2.
- The supplement tier-list video (week 5) is framed as "how much they matter (least)", with no buy links.

### 7.4 Before/after rules

1. **Same conditions or no comparison.** Same time (08:05), fasted, same spot, light setting, distance, lens, shirt and posture. No pump, no flexing, no sucking in, no tanning. No filters, beauty mode, AI retouching or warping.
2. **Date-stamped.** Every checkpoint photo has the slate shot (§2.3 #1). Keep the untouched originals archived.
3. **Show the trend, not the best day.** Use weekly and 7-day averages. If a checkpoint looks worse, post it.
4. **Plain results language on every before/after:**
   - **OST:** `One person · my results · yours will differ`
   - **Spoken:** "This is just me. I don't know what you'd get."
   - The FTC expects results claims to be typical or clearly qualified with what people generally achieve. We have no user data, so we say exactly that.
5. **No juxtaposition with a buy/download CTA in the same frame** as the side-by-side. The CTA comes later in the video, with the disclosure.
6. **Platform safety.** Keep it non-extreme: no body-shaming language ("disgusting", "fat me"), no extreme deficits, no "what I ate in a day: 800 kcal". Prefer a fitted-t-shirt side profile and tape close-ups for posted comparisons. Shirtless photos are for the private record unless he chooses otherwise.
7. **Red flags stop the story.** If anything on PLAN.md's "See a doctor if" list shows up, or weight drops faster than about 1% a week for weeks, he pauses the series and tells viewers he's seeing a doctor. Don't film the visit.

| **3-s hold / swipe-away rate** | TikTok "retention", IG "skip rate", YT "viewed vs swiped away" | Hook quality | ≥60% stay past 3 s |
| **Average % watched** | Native | Watch-through, the main ranking signal | ≥50% on 30–45 s posts |
| **Saves per 1k views** | Native | Usefulness | ≥10 (eating-out and breathing posts) |
| **Shares/sends per 1k views** | Native | Non-follower reach (especially IG) | ≥5 (myth posts) |
| **Follows per 1k views** | Native | Series pull | ≥3 |
| **Keyword comments (RESET/STRESS)** | ManyChat + manual count | Intent | ≥1% of comments → DMs |
| **Bio-link clicks** | Link page + UTMs | Traffic | 0.3–0.8% of views ([04](04-growth-roadmap.md) §2.2) |
| **Email signups** | Kit, by `utm_source` | The list is the real asset | 25–35% of landing visitors |
| **App activations** | Privacy-friendly analytics once shipped ([04](04-growth-roadmap.md) §3.8). Until then, "Open app" clicks on the link page as a proxy. | The north-star feeder | ≥45% of app opens activate |
| **Founder time** | Self-log | Sustainability | Filming ≤15 min/day; editing ≤30 min/day |

Also log, per post: format, hook type (number / contradiction / question / reveal), length, posting time, and whether the app was mentioned. That makes the post-21 review a sort-and-compare, not guesswork.

### 8.2 Weekly decision rules

- **Double down:** any post with ≥2× the platform's median views **or** ≥2× median saves/shares. Within 7 days make **3 variations**: same topic with new hooks; same hook with a new topic; and a "part 2". Turn its topic into next week's hero.
- **Fix the hook, not the topic:** if 3-s hold is below 50% on a format for 2 weeks, rewrite hooks for that format only. Put the number in frame 1, try a contradiction or a question.
- **Fix the CTA:** if views are healthy but keyword comments are under 0.5%, or link clicks are under 0.2%, test a different CTA (keyword vs bio vs "save this").

### 8.3 Review gates

**Post 21 (end of week 3): first real review.** Re-baseline the healthy signals above from our own medians.

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| A format sits in the bottom third on **both** watch-through and follows/1k | Replace it. Candidates: "Comment-reply with data", "Korean food swaps" (also tests KR appetite), "My week in 30 seconds". |
| Median views under ~300 on **all** platforms and follows/1k under 2 | **Pivot hook style and packaging, not the series.** Stronger number-first hooks, shorter (15–25 s), more myth/stitch posts riding trending cortisol videos. |
| One platform drives ≥60% of signups | Make it the primary edit target (its captions and length first). Others get straight reposts. |
| Myth posts dominate reach but Measure posts dominate follows | Normal. Keep both: myths bring strangers, measures make them stay. |

**Post 42 (end of week 6): conversion review.**
- **Email list under 100:** change the lead magnet framing (e.g. "84-day printable tracker" instead of "PDF plan") and the landing page.
- **A platform under 10% of signups** after 42 posts: move it to repost-only, with no platform-specific editing.

**Post 63 (week 9):** decide whether to plan a "Season 2" (maintenance, weeks 13–24) and when to start KR-first posting.

**Day 84:** a full retro. What to keep for KR. Which 10 videos to re-cut as the "best of" evergreen set.

**Hard stop rule.** If the founder is over the time budget for 2 weeks running, or the series is hurting his sleep or stress, cut to **5 posts a week**: drop the Sat and Thu formats first. A stressed founder filming a stress series is the one failure we can't post our way out of.
